Microneedling

Full-face microneedling is used to improve the look of wrinkles, surgical scars, stretch marks, lines around the lips, hyperpigmentation and acne scars.

For best results, Glo recommends a series of 2–4 treatments, spaced 4–6 weeks apart.

eMatrix

eMatrix Sublative Skin Resurfacing uses radiofrequency to stimulate collagen production, which helps improve the appearance of fine lines, wrinkles, acne scars and stretch marks.

It is one of the ways Glo works on texture at a deeper level — planned around your skin, and only recommended when it is genuinely the right fit.
